kap-watcher

Classifies Turkish public-company disclosures (KAP), extracts amount, date and counterparty, and writes Turkish and English summaries in which every number must appear in a verbatim source quote.

Realistic examples, not real filings. The six disclosures below are written in KAP style about fictional companies. They come from the independently written evaluation sets. Model output: replay (recorded gemma-4-31b-it, 2026-11-17). Watchlist: any ticker for dividend, M&A, legal and capital events, plus HENRJ for contracts. Not investment advice. Source and method on GitHub.
